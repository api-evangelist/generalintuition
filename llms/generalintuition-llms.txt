# General Intuition

> General Intuition is a frontier AI lab (founded 2025, spun out of Medal) building spatial-temporal world and action models that learn to perceive, anticipate, and act in space and time — trained primarily on gameplay data rather than the archive of human language. It is developing a commercial API for gaming, simulation, and robotics partners, and is backed by Khosla Ventures ($133.7M seed, October 2025; $320M Series A at a $2.3B valuation, 2026).
